walk workbench
checking the engine

Choose a floor, pick a house ticket or mark faces on the map, and read the walk the engine returns: the tour, the sweep it is measured against, and the seconds it takes.

This workbench talks to the northspan planner over its published HTTP interface. Both floors are fictional worked examples; every figure comes from the engine.

The floor

The engine publishes its floors with their tickets. Pick one and the map renders from the engine's own reading.

House tickets

A ticket is a worked cart. Walking one shows the engine's tour against its sweep.

Your own cart

Click faces on the map to build a cart. The walk button sends the list to the engine.
